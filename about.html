SOCIAL IMPACT

Creating positive, lasting social impact in the world

"As a player-focused company, we hope to create game experiences that are meaningful to players, and also hope to help improve the world they live in by serving and investing in the communities we’re a part of."

We founded Biot with the belief that a company of gamers, deeply committed to serving players, could deliver amazing game experiences. We believe that anyone with passion, vision, and perseverance can make a positive impact. As part of that ethos, we also believe that there are ways where we, as a company, can provide long-term value and positive impact to players, partners, Bioters, and the communities we serve.

Through corporate social responsibility, we hope to help build a more positive world for the players and our communities.
