OVERVIEW

An action-packed tactical shooter, which includes elements of precision shooting and the use of special powers provided to individual characters. Every match includes two teams competing against each other. The task of the players on both sides is to coordinate among themselves, control particular zones on the map, and use the weapons and powers available to win over the opponent. The gameplay in the video game is very appealing due to the combination of skills required from players and strategies employed in each particular game. Good aiming helps a lot but is not enough to defeat the competitor; players should also be ready for coordination among themselves and the ability to make proper decisions. BANORANT will be interesting for gamers fond of competition and fast pace of playing games. The players will find many opportunities to show their skills both individually and as part of the team.
