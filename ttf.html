OVERVIEW

Tactical Teamfights is an auto-battler strategy game where players draft champions, deploy them in battle, and use strategic thinking to outlast their opponents. With a rotating pool of champions from Legends of League, each match is a test of tactical decision-making. Players must adapt to changing circumstances, collect gold, and select the right combination of champions to build their team. Every match offers a unique experience, requiring both foresight and adaptability to rise to the top.
